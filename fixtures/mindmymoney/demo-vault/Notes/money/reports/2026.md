## What is surprising

- Electricity goes down from €118.00 to €94.00 a month (*NEUER TARIF*, a new tariff), €288.00 a year less.
- **A second home contents insurance starts**: Blauschild, €21.90 a month from 10 January, while Nordstern's home contents policy (€18.40 a month) is still being paid.
- Both Bildstrom memberships go up from €12.99 to €14.99 a month on the same day of the month they always charge; together €48.00 a year more.
- Nordstern's home contents insurance is charged for the last time on 8 March. For January, February and March both insurers were paid for home contents: €55.20 to Nordstern for those three months.
- A Ratenwerk instalment of €163.00 starts on the joint account (*Vorgang 40117*), the same amount on the 11th of April, May and June. The bank text does not say what was bought.
- This year report holds January to June only: the banks' exports end on 30 June 2026, and July to September 2026 were not offered by any of the three.
- Kaufhalle Nord is paid two ways: €1,831.20 in the shop and €1,364.77 for its delivery service, €3,195.97 together, 74% of Groceries.
- The bakery was paid 51 times (€396.15) and the two cafés 42 times (€442.59): small amounts, €838.74 together.
