## Where the money really goes, and what is still running

**The money is not all gone.** Over these twelve months €53,169.22 went out and €56,640.00 came in (Sam's salary, €2,480.00 a month, and your draw from Carter Studio, €2,240.00 a month). **Where it goes, in order:** Rent and service charges €20,640.00 (39%). **Groceries €9,053.07 (17%)**, about €754 a month, the second-largest group by a long way. Then Transport €3,171.00, Household bills €2,932.44, Holidays €2,200.40. **Subscriptions are €928.28 (1.7%)**: nine services, less than a tenth of what groceries cost.

**What is quietly still running.** Four of those nine subscriptions are two pairs:

- **Two Amazon Prime memberships.** amazon.co.uk Prime on your Meridian card (£8.99, about €10.60 a month, every month of the year read) and amazon.de Prime on the joint account (€8.99 a month since September 2025). €217.04 in these twelve months.

- And one that is new: **Warenlager Plus**, €4.99 a month on your card since December 2025, a free trial that turned into a subscription.

Home contents was also insured twice for three months, January to March 2026, Nordstern and Blauschild at once; Nordstern stopped after March.

**What you decided, 1 October 2026:** the joint account's Bildstrom (5512-887, never watched) is cancelled, ending 14.07.2026 by Bildstrom's own page. You are cancelling the UK Prime and Warenlager Plus (the forgotten trial) yourself, and writing to Nordstern for the €55.20. Everything else you keep.
