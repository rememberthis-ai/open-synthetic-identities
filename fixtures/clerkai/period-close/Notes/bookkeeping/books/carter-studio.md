Carter Studio UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Berlin. Alex Carter's design and
front-end consultancy — the company, not the club. German UG, small-business
bookkeeping, EÜR. Closed **monthly**, usually in the first week of the
following month.

Everything goes to **M. Fischer Steuerberatung** (kanzlei@fischer-steuerberatung.example),
who reads and replies in German — so the packet summary and the covering email
are written in German even though Alex works in English.

## What belongs to this set of books

Anything paid on the **Meridian Business** account or the **Meridian Business
card**. That is the whole rule for the overwhelming majority of the month: if
Meridian Business paid it, it is the studio's, and it does not need asking
about.

Alex also carries a personal **Meridian Everyday** card. Charges on that one are
*not* automatically the studio's — most are household, some are the studio's,
and a few are the climbing club's and get reimbursed. Those need asking about
unless a previous answer already covers the vendor.

Recurring on the Meridian Business card, all studio: Werkraum Kollektiv
(co-working desk, monthly, invoiced), Nimbus Cloud Hosting (metered, so the
amount moves), Fontrack Pro (design SaaS seat), Bürobedarf Kern (office
supplies, German receipts), RailLink (client travel), Brew & Bean (client
coffees and working lunches — these need the Anlass and the participants noted
or Fischer sends them back, cf. § 4 Abs. 5 Nr. 2 EStG).

Groceries at Supermercado Listo turn up on the business card most months and are
household, not the studio — but ask rather than assume, because office snacks
and client catering come from the same shop.

Stadtwerke Berlin is the flat's utility, never the studio's.

## Where the material comes from each period

The Meridian Business statement is exported from the Meridian web portal
(CSV). Client invoices go out from the studio's own numbering, INV-YYYY-MM, and
land in Gmail. Receipts are photographed as they happen and picked up from the
photo library.
