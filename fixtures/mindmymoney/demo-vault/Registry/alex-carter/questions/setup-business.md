## Question

Tell me a bit about yourself. Which banks and cards do you use, what worries you about money, and what would be most interesting to look at? If you already keep a budget or an old spreadsheet somewhere, point me at it and I'll start from the way you already sort things.

## Answer

There are three of us in Berlin: me, Sam and our daughter. My everyday card is with Meridian, Sam and I share the current account at Havelbank, and Sam has a card at Nordufer too. The money always seems to be gone and I honestly cannot say where. I think it is the groceries and all the little subscriptions. What I would most like to see is where it really goes over the year, and whether anything is quietly still running that we forgot about.
