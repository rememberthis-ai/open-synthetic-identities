# Money

⚠️ **This is a demo: a made-up household, not anybody's real money.** Alex Carter, Sam Okafor and
their daughter Mira are invented, and so is every bank, shop and company in these files except
Amazon, which appears only as statement text. The statements were generated, and then Mind My Money
ran an ordinary round on them: thirteen months, June 2025 to June 2026, three accounts, about a
thousand payments. Look around and press things. The banks and shops do not exist, so nothing can be fetched
from them, and nothing here is anybody's account.

What Mind My Money reads and writes, in plain markdown: `round.md` for the current round, `rows/` for every transaction named and sorted, one table per month, `evidence/` and `lines/` for what proved a name and what was fetched behind it, `reports/` for where the money went, one file per month, quarter and year, `ledger.md` for every recurring commitment and the running total, `items/` one file per commitment, `actions.md` for what was approved and verified, `raw/` for the exports taken from your banks. All of it stays on this computer.
